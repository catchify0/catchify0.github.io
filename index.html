chat_bubble Frequently Asked Questions

What is Catchify?

Catchify is a free Android app for streaming and downloading music, with no ads, no subscriptions, offline playback, custom playlists, an equalizer, lyrics, and support for 21+ languages including Tamil, Hindi, and Telugu.

Is Catchify free to use?

Yes. Catchify is completely free — no ads, no subscription plans, and no hidden costs. You can use it forever for free.

Is Catchify a good Spotify alternative?

Yes. Catchify is a free, ad-free alternative to Spotify for Android and iOS. Unlike Spotify, there are no subscription fees, no audio ads interrupting your music, no forced shuffle on mobile, and you can download unlimited songs offline at no cost.

Does Catchify have forced shuffle like Spotify free?

No. Catchify gives you 100% on-demand playback. You can pick any track, skip without limits, replay, and scrub smoothly without any forced shuffle restrictions.

Can I import my Spotify playlists to Catchify?

Yes! In Spotify, tap Share on any public playlist and copy the link. In Catchify, go to Playlists > Import via Link, paste the link, and Catchify will match and load your full playlist for free ad-free streaming and offline download.

Can I play music in the background when the screen is locked?

Yes. Catchify fully supports background playback with lock-screen media controls on both Android and iOS devices, so your music keeps playing even when your phone is in your pocket.

Is Catchify a good JioSaavn / Gaana alternative?

Yes. Catchify is completely free with no ads — a great replacement for JioSaavn, Gaana, or Wynk Music, especially for Indian regional language users. It supports Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and 18+ more languages with curated music suggestions.

What is the best free music app for Android without ads?

Catchify is one of the best free, ad-free music apps for Android. It's open-source, requires no subscription, and offers offline downloads, equalizer, lyrics, and 21+ language support — making it a top choice among free music apps.

Can I listen to music offline with Catchify?

Yes. Download songs and full playlists for offline listening — no internet needed after download.

What languages does Catchify support?

The interface supports 21+ languages, with curated music suggestions for Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Kannada, Punjabi, Malayalam, Gujarati, Urdu, Odia, Assamese, Sanskrit, and Konkani.

Is Catchify open source?

Yes. Catchify is fully open-source. The source code is available on GitHub.

How do I install Catchify on Android?

Tap the Download Android (.apk) button on this page — the APK downloads directly. Open it on your Android device and allow installation from unknown sources if prompted.

How do I install Catchify on iOS (iPhone / iPad)?

Since Catchify is 100% free, open-source, and not distributed through Apple's App Store, you can sideload the Catchify.ipa in minutes onto any iPhone or iPad without jailbreaking. Follow the detailed steps below:

Method 1: Sideloadly (Recommended for PC & Mac)

Recommended
Prerequisites:
  • A computer running Windows 10/11 or macOS.
  • Windows users: Ensure iTunes & iCloud (or the Apple Devices app) are installed directly from Apple so USB drivers are active.
  • USB cable connecting your iPhone/iPad to your computer.
Step-by-Step Installation:
  1. Download Sideloadly: Download and install Sideloadly from sideloadly.io.
  2. Connect your iPhone: Plug your iPhone into your computer via USB. If prompted with "Trust This Computer?" on your iPhone, tap Trust and enter your device passcode.
  3. Load Catchify.ipa: Open Sideloadly. Make sure your iPhone is selected in the iDevice dropdown. Drag and drop the downloaded Catchify-v2.4.2.ipa into the large IPA area in Sideloadly.
  4. Sign with Apple ID: Enter your Apple ID email in the Apple ID field and click Start. When prompted, enter your Apple ID password and 2FA authentication code. (This is sent securely to Apple to generate a free 7-day personal developer certificate).
  5. Trust the Certificate on iPhone: Once Sideloadly finishes (shows Done), Catchify will appear on your home screen. On your iPhone:
    Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > tap your Apple ID email under Developer App > tap Trust "[Your Apple ID]".
  6. Enable Developer Mode (iOS 16, 17, 18+):
    Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > Toggle ON. Tap Restart when prompted. After the phone restarts, unlock it and tap Turn On > enter passcode.
  7. Ready to Enjoy: Open Catchify from your home screen and start streaming or downloading ad-free!
💡 Automatic Wi-Fi Refresh: Click the Wi-Fi icon in Sideloadly next to your connected device. As long as your computer and iPhone are connected to the same local Wi-Fi, Sideloadly will automatically renew your 7-day certificate wirelessly in the background—no weekly USB cable needed!

Method 2: AltStore or TrollStore

  • AltStore (Universal for all iOS versions):
    Install AltServer on your PC or Mac, install AltStore onto your iPhone, and use the "Open in AltStore" feature to sideload Catchify-v2.4.2.ipa directly.
  • TrollStore (Permanent – iOS 14.0 – 16.6.1 / 17.0):
    If your device and iOS version support TrollStore, simply open the downloaded IPA file with TrollStore for permanent installation with zero certificate expiration and no PC needed!
  • Jailbreak (Filza):
    Install with AppSync Unified on any jailbroken device without restrictions.
